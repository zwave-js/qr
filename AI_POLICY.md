# AI Policy

We support using AI tools, including large language models, when contributing
to Z-Wave JS projects. However, you are responsible for every contribution you
submit. Maintainers are responsible for every contribution they merge and
release. We hold a high standard for all contributions to our projects.

Our maintainers dedicate their time and expertise to reviewing contributions.
Submitting AI-generated content that you have not personally reviewed and
understood wastes that time and will not be accepted.

## Autonomous agents

**We do not allow autonomous agents to contribute to our projects.** We will
close pull requests or issues that we believe were created autonomously. We may
also mark automated comments as spam. This includes contributions that bypass
the provided issue or pull request templates.

## Communication on issues, pull requests, and code reviews

You may use AI tools to help you write. However, do not let tools post
unreviewed content on your behalf. Keep responses to the minimum needed to
communicate your intent. We may hide comments that we believe are unreviewed AI
output.

If you open a pull request, you must be able to explain the proposed changes in
your own words. This includes the pull request description and responses to
questions. If you use AI to help generate a pull request summary, you must
review it for technical accuracy.

**Do not use AI to generate answers to questions from maintainers.** You must
understand and be able to explain your own work. You may use AI to improve
grammar or clarity. The substance of your responses must be your own.

If you include context from an AI interaction in a comment, put it in a quote
block by prefixing it with `>`. Disclose that the quoted text came from an AI
interaction. Add your own commentary to explain its relevance and
implications. Do not share long excerpts.

## Non-native English speakers

We understand that AI is useful when communicating in a language that is not
your native language. You may use AI to improve the grammar or clarity of text
you wrote yourself. If you use AI to translate your comments, make sure the
translation accurately reflects your intent. Including your original text in a
details block shows the effort behind your contribution. It also helps
maintainers verify the translation and keeps the conversation readable.

## Code and documentation contributions

AI can help write code and documentation. However, our open source projects
require a human contributor who understands the work produced by AI.

You must personally review and understand every contribution before submitting
it. You must be able to explain every change in a pull request you submit. We
will close pull requests that appear to contain unreviewed AI output without
reviewing them.

## Our use of AI

Some Z-Wave JS projects use AI tools to assist with code reviews, issue
triaging, reporting, and other project management tasks. These tools may leave
comments on pull requests or issues. Automated comments can be wrong.

Treat a comment from an AI tool like any other review comment. If you believe
it is incorrect, say so. A brief explanation is enough. Maintainers have the
final say. Ask a maintainer if you are unsure.

## Enforcement

We will close contributions that do not follow this policy. Repeated violations
may result in the contributor being blocked from Z-Wave JS projects. If you
believe your contribution was closed in error, contact a maintainer to discuss
it.

This policy is adapted from the
[Home Assistant AI policy](https://developers.home-assistant.io/docs/ai_policy)
published by the Open Home Foundation.
